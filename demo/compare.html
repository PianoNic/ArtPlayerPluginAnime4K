Open an anime episode from your disk (or drop it on the player). Pause on a frame with clear line art and drag the slider: left of the line is the plain video, right of it the Anime4K output. The x2 upscale only runs when the player is more than 1.2x the video's resolution; otherwise you see the restore/sharpen step alone. Fullscreen a 720p or 1080p episode for the strongest effect. Keys: S split on/off, 1–5 modes.
